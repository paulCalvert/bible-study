Priscilla & Aquila: Marriage, Submission & Oneness

CMBC Men's Breakfast  |  September 2026
Core Thread: What does a Godly marriage look like? Priscilla and Aquila are our model — two becoming one through love and submission.
Cheat-sheet format — keywords and fragments to prompt, not read from. Scripture already on the slides is not repeated here (just cited); slides are presented alongside.

Segment 1 — Meeting Priscilla & Aquila  (~11-11.5 min — timed from an actual practice run | Acts 15–18)
  • Paul's Rough 2nd Missionary Journey (~6.5-7 min)
    • Barnabas split → Silas/Timothy → picks up Luke (15:36-41; 16:1-3, 10)
    • Blocked Asia/Ephesus → redirected straight to Greece (16:6-8)
    • Philippi — flogged, jailed, first time. Leaves Luke to pastor (16:19-40)
    • Thessalonica — some success, opposition → leaves to protect them, 45mi to Berea (17:1-9)
    • Berea — eager reception → Thessalonica mob marches 45mi, chases him out (17:10-15). Leaves Silas/Timothy (maybe to keep teaching), alone to Athens
    • Athens — Areopagus invite ("cool kids table") (17:16-34) — sermon cut off mid-climax ("you're boring, don't call us"), doesn't wait, alone to Corinth
    • Corinth's reputation — "no good Jewish man" territory, modern-day Sodom, slang for sleeping around. Maybe his parents even warned him growing up (18:1) (no Greek-term detail live anymore, just the slang line)
    • Feeling by now: healing wounds, missing Luke/Silas/Timothy/Barnabas, "did I blow the chance of a lifetime," wanted Athens/Ephesus not here, "maybe I'm just better off making tents"
    • Ray of light — meets Aquila & Priscilla, already Christians (18:1-3) — how they met not spelled out live anymore, just that he stays with them; settles a full year, builds the church
    • Landing: Silas/Timothy catch up too — this is the turning point, P&A bring life right when he's in a dark place
  • What Do We Even Know About Them? (~3.5 min)
    • Christian how? Pontus — Pentecost (2:9) — maybe a Christian longer than Paul was — or Peter's later ministry (1 Pet 1:1)
    • More than average tentmakers — multiple home churches, moved constantly. Real means implied
    • Mixed marriage? Aquila = Jew explicit, Priscilla never. Prisca = old Roman name (watch it live — easy to slip and say "Prissa"), named first more often (not settled, handout)
    • Landing: whoever they were before — none of it mattered once they were one
  • Arrival in Ephesus (~45 sec-1 min) — (18:18-21) pastors Corinth ~1yr → finally Ephesus, brings P&A. Stays briefly, will return for 3rd missionary trip; they remain, help establish the church
    • Maybe Spirit blocked Paul earlier because He wanted P&A there first
    • Landing: impact already visible — Paul later writes they've helped him so much in the ministry

Segment 3 — Be Unified  (~14.75-15.75 min — trimmed slavery to one live sentence, saving ~30-45 sec | Eph 5)

    • Landing: always together, always same verse, always doing God's work — never divided
  • Ephesians 5:22-33 — Submit and Love (~45-50 sec) — Eph 5:22-33. Wives submit/husbands love. Submission defined here — respect leadership, don't object to authority
    • Landing: love is more nuanced — next
  • What Is Love? (~35-40 sec) — not lovey-dovey, proactive/self-giving (John 3:16 tie-in: "for God so loved the world...")

  • What Builds and Breaks Unity? (~55 sec-1 min) — Eph 5:1-2, 5:21. Opposite submission = defiance. Opposite love = apathy ("for God so didn't care" riff — not how the verse goes)

  • Scriptural Tension (~50-55 sec) — (5:1-2, 5:21) same letter, same chapter — everyone submits AND loves, no gender restriction
