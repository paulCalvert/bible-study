Priscilla & Aquila: Marriage, Submission & Oneness

CMBC Men's Breakfast  |  September 2026
Core Thread: What does a Godly marriage look like? Priscilla and Aquila are our model — two becoming one through love and submission.
Cheat-sheet format — keywords and fragments to prompt, not read from. Scripture already on the slides is not repeated here (just cited); slides are presented alongside.
Intro  (~5 min, before prayer)
  • The Challenge (~2 min) — husband models, mostly warnings
    • David — great man, bad husband. Multiple marriages, never dealt with (Michal)
    • Abraham — better, same failings too (Abraham study callback)
    • Peter — Google suggested, not much known
    • Boaz — courtship model, not marriage
  • The Turn (~1.5 min) — "Aquila" kept surfacing, passed over. Can't separate Aquila from Priscilla
    • Isolating the husband = separating what God joined (Matthew 19:6, on slide)
  • The Payoff Fact (~45 sec) — (Acts 18:2, 18, 26; Rom 16:3; 1 Cor 16:19; 2 Tim 4:19) named 6x, always same verse, never apart
  • Preview (~30 sec) — pray → Segment 1
Segment 1 — Meeting Priscilla & Aquila  (~11.5-13.5 min | Acts 15–18)
  • Paul's Rough 2nd Missionary Journey (~5.5-6 min)
    • Barnabas split → Silas/Timothy → picks up Luke (15:36-41; 16:1-3, 10)
    • Blocked Asia/Ephesus, then Bithynia (16:6-8) — "Mexico trip → Cuba" bit
    • Philippi — flogged, jailed, first time. Leaves Luke to pastor (16:19-40)
    • Thessalonica — some success, opposition → Berea at night (17:1-9)
    • Berea — eager reception → Thessalonica mob marches 45mi, chases him out (17:10-15) — "2.5 days mad" bit. Leaves Silas/Timothy, alone to Athens
    • Athens — Areopagus sermon, sneered out mid-argument (17:16-34) — doesn't wait, alone to Corinth
    • Corinth — not exotic to him, but the reputation (18:1)
      • "Corinthianize" — real verb, fornication. Insult if not from Corinth
      • Caveat, not live: older vs. rebuilt Corinth
    • Feeling by now: healing wounds, missing Luke/Silas/Timothy/Barnabas, Athens doubt, no status here
    • Jewish quarter, hoping to stay/regroup (18:2-3) — Aquila's shop, before Paul brings up Jesus, Aquila asks HIM
  • Introducing Priscilla (~1-1.5 min) — (18:2) hit it off fast — "meet my wife"
    • 18:2 = only place names sit apart (~10 words). Every other mention adjacent
  • What Do We Even Know About Them? (~4 min)
    • Christian how? Pontus — Pentecost (2:9) or Peter's later ministry (1 Pet 1:1)
    • More than average tentmakers — multiple home churches, moved constantly. Real means implied
    • Mixed marriage? Aquila = Jew explicit, Priscilla never. Prisca = old Roman name, named first more often (not settled, handout)
    • Landing: whoever they were before — none of it mattered once they were one
  • Arrival in Ephesus (~1-2 min) — (18:18-21) pastors Corinth ~1yr → finally Ephesus, brings P&A. Stays briefly; they remain, help establish the church
    • Maybe Spirit blocked Paul earlier because He wanted P&A there first
Round 1
Has God ever sent you a "Priscilla and Aquila" — someone who showed up right when you needed support most?
Segment 2 — Priscilla, Aquila, and Apollos  (~17-18 min | Acts 18:24-28, 1 Tim 2)
  • Part 1 — The Apollos Story
  • Transition (~15 sec) — Paul gone, P&A the leaders left behind. Hottest segment — give it the time/respect it deserves
  • A New Hotshot Arrives (~30 sec) — (18:24-25) Apollos — gifted, gaps
  • Corrected Privately, Received Gladly (~1 min) — (18:26-28; 1 Cor 3:6) taken aside privately, honorable route. Not defensive, becomes teacher in Corinth
    • Great team
  • Was Priscilla the Lead Teacher? (~1 min) — name order, Priscilla first (18:26). Same flip as Barnabas/Saul → Paul/Barnabas (Acts 13). At least co-leader, maybe primary
    • Name order still matters today — first name = credit
  • Is This a Problem? (~30 sec) — divisive today. 1 Tim 2 sounds like it disagrees. Let's read it
  • Part 2 — Why Cultural Context Matters
  • Read the Passage (~1 min) — 1 Timothy 2:9-15, on slide
  • But Wait, I Have Seen Women Teachers? (~30 sec) — Budde (inauguration), Pastor Ashley Bell. Why, if forbidding?
    • Not just modern — 1 Tim written TO Ephesus, Priscilla a fixture there. Original hearers: "what about her?"
  • Cultural Context as a Tool (~45 sec) — not an excuse, a tool to read as the original audience would
    • Hermeneutics — Segment 3 picks it back up
  • Why Ephesus Mattered (~1 min) — imperial cult, banking, governor's seat, Asia's leading city
    • Confirmed: 1 Cor (FROM), 1&2 Tim (TO), Eph, Rev (1st of 7)
    • Strong-argument: John (post-Patmos tradition), Titus (Crete parallel), Phil/Col/Phlm (Ephesian-imprisonment theory)
  • Who Was Artemis? (~1 min) — 2nd-most-titled deity, behind only Zeus. Pausanias — "above all the gods" (4.31.8)
    • Temple: 4x Parthenon, 127 columns — Wonder of the World
  • Part 3 — Reading 1 Timothy 2 Through Ephesus's Eyes
  • The Hair Instruction (~1 min) — (2:9) πλέγμασιν ~ Artemis procession hairstyle (Xenophon 1.2.6) — specific look, not fashion generally
  • "Saved Through Childbearing" (~1 min) — (2:15) Artemis = virgin AND childbirth goddess — safe delivery or painless death
    • "Savior" title clusters Ephesus/Crete letters only
    • Mortality ~1-2.5% ("1 in 50") — 10x today's global rate, ~100x wealthy-nation. Say "10x" out loud
    • Landing: Artemis isn't Savior. Jesus is
  • "Adam Was Formed First" (~1 min) — (2:13) firstborn = Artemis title too (twin Apollo). Maybe restoring interdependence, not hierarchy
    • 2nd angle: Gnostic heresy, Eve-made-first claim (Kroegers)
  • "I Do Not Permit a Woman to Teach" (~2.5 min) — (2:12) hardest verse, multiple things going on
    • αὐθεντεῖν — rare, could mean "dominate/usurp," not just "exercise authority"
    • Artemis cult — competitive priestesses, real status
    • Wealthy female benefactors — leverage habit carried over
    • Magic history — book-burning (19:18-19). "Busybodies" (5:13) = magic?
    • 2:8 — men corrected too ("without anger/quarreling") — not women-only
    • Landing: specific fire, not universal rule
  • Part 4 — Back to Priscilla & Aquila
  • Does That Fully Resolve It? (~1 min) — changes the read, doesn't it? Not fully — still debated. Look at how the men handled it
  • Aquila Welcomed It (~1 min) — didn't cut Priscilla out — welcomed working alongside her
  • Apollos Welcomed It (~1 min) — (18:26) rare to welcome correction at all, rarer from a woman
  • Emulate the Men (~1 min)
    • Aquila — welcome your wife's strengths, real opportunity to lead
    • Apollos — stay humble, learn from whomever God speaks through
Round 2
When was a time God spoke to you through an unexpected person?
Optional follow-up: What almost kept you from listening?
Segment 3 — Marriage, Submission & Oneness  (~15.5 min | Eph 5, Gen 2:24)
  • Transition (~30 sec) — covered who/journey/Apollos. What about their marriage?
  • Ephesians 5 and the Tension (~2 min) — Ephesians 5:22-33, on slide
    • Jesus = "Savior" here too
    • P&A never named in Ephesians — but named in 2 Tim 4:19 (Timothy in Ephesus, years later)
    • Tension w/ Gal 3:28 — "no male and female"
    • Landing: if no gender distinction, why here?
  • Inspired Some Writing — Have You Read Any? (~1-2 min) — Love & Respect, Wild at Heart, others pictured. "Men = Y, Women = X" boilerplate
    • Genuinely helped people — men (Wild at Heart), marriages (Love & Respect)
    • Personal: marriage-prep class, encouraged to read L&R. Lauren asked pastor: "does this say I don't need respect?" — didn't read it
  • Mutual Submission + Timeless vs. Cultural Truths (~1.5 min) — (5:21) "submit to one another" — no gender restriction, only v.22 narrows
    • Transcends culture like Romans 8:38-39 (on slide)
    • What follows = culturally tied, read differently
    • Same pattern for love: 5:1-2 general → v.25 narrows to husbands
  • How Do We Navigate These Cultural Truths? (~1 min) — redemptive arc: progressive-then can look regressive now
  • Levirate Marriage (~2 min) — (Deut 25:5-10, on slide) widow marries brother
    • Protective then — no income, vulnerable, kids = weight
    • Not redemptive now — vulnerabilities changed
  • Why Paul Wrote What He Wrote (~1.5 min) — 1 Corinthians 9:22, on slide — could've pushed countercultural, chose reconciliation over revolution
    • Same instinct as Segment 2 — work within the culture
  • Reading Ephesians 5 Now (~2 min)
    • Mutual submission = everyone; marriage specifics = direction of existing strength
    • NOT "rule/lead" — only "love" + steep accountability. Stretches, doesn't snap
    • Today — more freedom/representation, not bound to Ephesus-specific advice
    • Landing: love/submission themselves NOT culturally bound
  • Two Become One (~2 min) — Genesis 2:24, on slide — marriage's purpose: unity
  • What Builds and Breaks Unity?
    • Opposite submission = defiance. Opposite love = indifference
    • Headline = husbands love / wives submit — but both need the other too
    • Edification tool
    • Slide: Eph 5:1-2, 5:21 — submission/love for everyone; defiance/apathy break it
    • Personal: no problem submitting — apathy/withdrawal is MY break point (Lauren)
  • What Do You Need to Work On? (~1 min) — 2 scenarios, straight from slide: husband "don't need to submit" / wife "don't need to feel." Paul(s)* respond
    • *Apostle Paul + Paul Calvert joke
  • Back to Priscilla & Aquila (~1 min) — Priscilla = co-leader, maybe primary, not just the Apollos episode
    • Landing: whichever held more status, essentially one. No longer two, but one
  • Application For Us (~1 min) — differs by relationship/context, but submit + love = everyone. Work it out together
Round 3
We all need to become better at love and submission. Which do you think YOU need to work on? And who is a good partner to work on this with?
