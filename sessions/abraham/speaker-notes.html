Abraham: Growing in Trust — A Lifetime of Walking with God

CMBC Men's Breakfast  |  July 2026
Core Thread: How does a man learn to walk with God? Abraham is our model.
Intro  (~2 min, before prayer)
  • What do we know about Abraham?
  • Abraham is important. 2 of the biggest faiths in the world are Abrahamic faiths
  • Story is incredible. Highlights only — take-home notes for the full picture
  • Not remarkable — just a guy trying to figure out how to be friends with God. No Bible, no godly family, no brothers in the faith
  • "Although we cannot be lawgivers, kings, generals, or prophets, we can be what Abram was — a man who heard God and believed that God can be trusted to do what he says he will do, and based his entire life on that conviction." — Boice
  • Pray → Section 1
Section 1 — Learning More About God  (~13 min | Gen 11–13)
  • Setting the Scene (~2 min)
    • First meet him as Abram — name change comes later
    • Ur, pre-Babylon. Wealth, privilege, close-knit family
    • Has it all: house, family, money, beautiful wife
    • One thing missing: no kids. Big deal back then
    • Newlyweds-ish — starting to wonder
  • The Call (~2 min)
    • God shows up, speaks into his fear — promises kids
    • Calls him to leave home, people, family — culturally unnatural
    • Back then: you did NOT leave your clan
    • Trust God, not house / culture / family
  • Haran: Half-Obedience (~3 min)
    • Gets stuck in Haran for 50 years. Brought his dad and could not finish the journey
    • Terah dies, and God calls Abram again. This time Abram follows God
    • Learns: God is patient and wants us to come back to him
    • "Once my career..." / "Once the right girl..." / "Once March Madness is over..."
    • Called twice — see notes
    • Take-home notes have the reasoning for why scholars estimate ~50 years
  • Good Season (~1 min)
    • In Canaan. Following, not settling. Building altars
    • Learning to listen and follow
  • Egypt (~5 min)
    • Famine. Could ask God — goes to Egypt instead
    • Sarai is beautiful. It's in the text. Afraid they'll kill him for her
    • "Say you're my sister." Half true. Thinks he has it under control. He does not — she is taken to Pharaoh
    • Has Abram screwed up everything?
    • God is faithful to Abram. Disease on Pharaoh's household, Pharaoh returns Sarai and pays Abram, and Abram is kicked out
    • Learns: God doesn't fail His promises even when Abram does
    • Returns to Bethel — back to where he last had relationship with God
Round 1 — Choose one
What have you said you need to take care of before you make time for God?
— or —
Have you walked into a bad decision thinking you had it under control? How did that go?
Section 2 — Two Steps Forward, One Step Back  (~13 min | Gen 13–16)
  • Aftermath of Egypt (~2 min)
    • Back in Canaan, following God. New problem from Egypt: too much money
    • Jordan Valley (lush) vs. arid. Lot picks Jordan. Camps near Sodom
    • Lot: wealth owns him. Abram owns his wealth
    • "I trust God now. Even in the desert, God will be there"
  • War, Rescue, King of Sodom (~3 min)
    • War. Invading army. Lot captured. Money didn't protect him
    • Abram rides out, wins, gets everything back
    • Battle details + Melchizedek — see notes, no time
    • King of Sodom: "Keep the loot, give me my people." Abram declines
    • Pharaoh paying him irked him — not letting a king take credit for God's blessings
    • Growth: Abram is trusting God to provide instead of the world
  • The Hinge (~4 min)
    • God speaks promises. Abram: "Where are the kids?"
    • "Don't look down. Look up." Stars. "You'll have that many."
    • Gen 15:6 — "Abram believed God, credited as righteousness"
    • Hinge of salvation. Paul, James, NT writers all quote it
    • Learns: God can be trusted
    • Covenant ceremony — see notes
  • Hagar (~4 min)
    • "He's got this now." He does not
    • Sarai despairs. Recommends Hagar
    • Not unusual culturally — servant's kids counted as wife's
    • Personal: 3 years trying to have kids. 60+ years for them. I get it
    • Hagar gets pregnant. [Confused yay wave]
    • Sarai knows she's the problem. Rivalry. Hagar driven out while pregnant
    • God redeemed the mess
    • Learns: God doesn't need us to solve the problems he said he will solve
Round 2
Two steps forward, one step back. Where do you see that pattern in your own walk with God?
Section 3 — Walking Steadier, But Not Perfect  (~13 min | Gen 17–21)
  • Circumcision + The Name (~5 min)
    • 29 years since the promise. New covenant sign — circumcision
    • Gen 17:23 — that very day. No delay. Compare to Haran
    • New name: Abraham. "Father of multitudes." At 99 with no son by Sarah
    • Shaq story
    • Growth: Abram obeys God immediately and fully
  • Abimelech (~5 min)
    • On an upward trajectory. And he fumbles — same mistake as Egypt
    • God protects Sarah again. Appears to Abimelech in a dream
    • Paid off again. No moral ground to refuse
    • Same lesson twice. God was patient. He is patient with us
    • Learns: I can fail in the same ways again and again, and God will still take me back
  • Isaac (~3 min)
    • God kept every word
    • "As he had said." "What he had promised." "At the very time."
    • Doubt, schemes, failure — none of it moved the promise
    • Failed early, late, after his biggest growth moments. Kept walking. God kept every word
    • Learns: God keeps his promises
Round 3
What are some things you think you should be better at or know better by now?
Optional follow-up: Has God been patient with you in that area — shown up anyway even when you kept stumbling?
Close — The Sacrifice of Isaac  (~5 min | Gen 22)
  • After everything. Isaac finally in his arms. God asks for it all back
  • Gen 22:2 — "Take your son, your only son Isaac, whom you love, and offer him"
  • Not blind faith — 60–70 years of evidence. Abraham would have failed this earlier in his walk
  • Final exam: "Do you trust me with all of it?" He said yes
  • God stopped him. Ram in the thicket. Kept His word one more time
  • This was never about how great Abraham was — it is about how great God is
