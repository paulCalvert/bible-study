Ephesus, Artemis & 1 Timothy 2 1 Tim 2:9–15
Ephesus's cult of Artemis as cultural background for the dress, silence, and Adam/Eve instructions in 1 Tim 2:9–15. Includes a confirmed Greek word study (πλέγμασιν, πεπλεγμένη, ἐμπλοκῆς, αὐθεντεῖν), named scholarly positions (Kroeger & Kroeger, Gritz, Hoag), and background on Artemis as Ephesus's civic and childbirth goddess.
