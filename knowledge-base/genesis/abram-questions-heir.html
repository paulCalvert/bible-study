Abram Questions God About an Heir
Genesis 15:2–5. God has just promised Abram a great reward — and Abram's response is a direct question: what can you give me if I have no son? Notes on the difference between honest complaint brought to God and the murmuring that never reaches him at all.

Notes

Abram brought his complaint directly to God — most people just murmur Boice 536
"Sometimes we do not demand that God give an accounting, but we do the opposite: we do not raise the question at all... We murmur and mutter. We are unhappy with the way things are, but we do not go to God. Abram laid this matter before God. He said, 'Lord, why is this happening? What are you doing? I don't have children, and I don't see how your promises can be fulfilled without them.' God answered his servant and gave Abram a promise he depended on for many years."

Boice identifies two failure modes for the struggling believer: demanding an accounting from God in anger, or never bringing the grievance to God at all — just murmuring quietly and staying stuck. Abram did neither. He went directly to God with an honest question, stated plainly, and God responded with one of the most important promises in Scripture (Gen 15:5 — "so shall your offspring be"). The honest prayer opened the door to the answer.
